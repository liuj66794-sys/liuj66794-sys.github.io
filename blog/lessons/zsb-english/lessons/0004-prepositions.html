介词 Prepositions

对应教材上册 P55–P68 · 介词表示名词、代词与句中其它词的关系。13 组介词辨析是考试重头戏。

考点地图 · 点击跳转
  1. 分类与三要素 —— 简单/复合/短语/分词介词；介词后接什么、何时省略
  2. 11 个常见介词 —— by、for、of、against、at、from、on、over、under、with、as
  3. 13 组介词辨析（考试重头戏）—— above/over/on、across/through、besides/except、in front of 等

一、介词三要素

介词的分类（教材 P55 思维导图）：简单介词（on）、复合介词（into）、短语介词（according to）、分词介词（concerning）。

三要素 1. 介词后 + 名词 / 动名词 doing / 宾格：
I am looking forward to your reply.（介+n.）期待你的回复。
She is afraid of swimming.（介+doing）她害怕游泳。
2. 地点副词前省略介词：go home（不说 to home）
3. 介词不可单独使用

二、11 个常见介词核心用法

介词核心用法例句
by在旁边；靠、通过（方法）；不迟于；被（被动语态）stand by the window；make a living by selling...；return by Monday；written by an American writer
for为了；向、往（去向）；支持（↔against）；因为；对于；持续一段时间prepare for the exam；The train is for Beijing.；famous for hot springs；wait for two hours
of所属（B of A = A 的 B）；of + great + 抽象名词 = very + 同根形容词；of + no + 抽象名词 = not + 同根形容词the secret of success；It is of great value. = very valuable；He is a man of no importance. = He is not an important man.
against倚、靠；反对（↔for）；以……为背景his back against the wall；Flowers look better against green leaves.
at在（小地点）；在（时刻/年龄）；以（速度）；朝、向arrive at the airport；run at 120 mph；Don't laugh at me!
from来自（hear from sb. 收到某人的来信）；由……制成（看不出原料）；使免受（prevent from 阻止）a letter from her mother；Wine is made from rice.；These books were saved from damage.
on在表面上；说明活动或状态；具体某一天on the desk；on holiday 休假中 / on fire 着火了 / on duty 值班；on May 15th / on Tuesday
over悬在正上方；到处；在……期间；越过fly over the woods；all over the country；jump over the wall
under在下面（↔over）；少于、未满Children under 6 are admitted free.
with和……在一起；带有；用（工具器官）；随着；with + n. + 宾补（独立主格）She lived with her mother.；a country with a long history；see with our eyes；With the development of...；Her father went out with his cap on.（戴着帽子出去了）
as作为、当作Don't treat me as a child. / As for me,... 至于我

前置课补充：off 离开/关闭（step off the train / turn off the lights）· about 关于 · to 到达/目的 · in 里面（in 2025 / in June / in summer）· out of 外面

辨析：be made of / from of 看得出原料：The kite is made of paper.
from 看不出原料：The paper is made from wood.
辨析：on / in the wall、tree on the wall 挂在墙表面 / in the wall 嵌在墙里面（如窗户）
on the tree 长在树上（树叶果实）/ in the tree 落在树上（外来物，如猫）

三、13 组介词辨析（重头戏）

1. 在上面：above / over / on

词区别反义词
above相对水平高度，不一定正上方below
over垂直正上方under
on与表面接触beneath

2. 穿过：across / over / through / past

across 表面穿过（walk across the road）· over 上方越过（jump over the wall）· through 内部穿过（through the forest）· past 从旁经过（walk past the shop）

3. 之间：between / among

between...and... 两者之间 · among 三者及以上中间

4–5. at / on / in 表地点与时间

表地点（教材 P64）：at the airport（小地点/某一点）→ He is in the swimming pool（空间内部）→ The cats are on the sofa（表面上）。

时间口诀 大于一天要用 in（in May / in 2025 / in summer）；等于一天要用 on（on Sunday / on May 15th）；小于一天要用 at（at 7 o'clock）
三长（in the morning / afternoon / evening）两短（at night / at noon）特殊记
注意on + 修饰词 + morning：on a cold morning；on Sunday morning

6. 方法手段：by / with / in / through

介词用法例
by交通工具、通信方式（单数名词前不加冠词）by air / by car / by post
with有形工具、身体器官see with our eyes
in语言、材料in English / in blue ink / in cash
through抽象途径master English through more practice

7. 除了：besides / except / except for / but / apart from

词区别
besides除……之外（还）——宾语包括在内
except除……之外——宾语排除在外（同类之间）
except for整体肯定 + 局部否定（非同类）
but常配 nobody / none / nothing / all / everyone 等
apart from可代替 besides / except for

Everyone is present except Helen. / The composition is good except for a few spelling mistakes.

8. by the end of / at the end of / in the end

短语含义时态搭配例句
by the end of到……为止（仅时间）过去完成时或将来时He will come by the end of this month.
at the end of在……尽头（可指地点和时间）一般过去时/将来时He came back at the end of March.
in the end最终 = finally，单用——In the end, the parents found their kids.

9. in front of / in the front of

in the front of 在……前部（范围内）：He is in the front of the car.（坐在前排）
in front of 在……前面（范围外）：His wife is in front of the car!（车外的前面）

10. by / before（时间点）

by = "不迟于某时，包括某时"，口诀"到哪哪完"：将来时间配将来时，过去时间配过去完成时：
I'll finish eating by nine o'clock. 我会在九点前吃完饭。
I had learned more than 2000 words by the end of last term.
before 在某时之前，不包括某时：
Several months before our trip, Wang Wei and I went to the library.

11. like / as

like 表相似："像……一样"（He looks like his father.）；as 表身份、职业："作为"（He works as a waiter.）

12. because / because of

because 连词接完整句子：She didn't go to school because she was ill. 她没去上学，因为她生病了。
because of 介词短语接名词/动名词：They delayed the trip because of the storm. 因为暴风雨，他们推迟了旅行。
同组：due to、thanks to。

13. of one's own / on one's own

of one's own 属于自己的（We have a library of our own.）；on one's own 独自地（She does the work on her own.）

易错清单

高频易错 1. on the tree 长在树上 / in the tree 落在树上；on the wall 挂表面 / in the wall 嵌墙内
2. be made of 看得出原料 / be made from 看不出
3. by + 交通工具单数不加冠词（by taxi ✔ / by a taxi ✘）
4. besides 加 / except 减 / except for 修细节 / but 配 no-all-every 系列
5. by the end of + 过去完成时或将来时；in the end 单用
6. in front of 范围外 / in the front of 范围内
7. on + 修饰词 + morning；但 in the morning、at night
8. of + great + 抽象名词 = very + 形容词
9. because 接句子 / because of 接名词
10. like 表相似 / as 表身份

课堂练习

口诀卡

本课全部口诀 三要素：介词不单独用，后面接名/代/doing；地点副词前免介
时间：大于一天 in，等于一天 on，小于一天 at；三长两短特殊记
树上墙上：长/挂表面用 on，落/嵌里面用 in
"除了"五兄弟：besides 加，except 减，except for 修细节，but 配否定，apart from 两者都能换
by the end of：到哪哪完，过去完成/将来时
