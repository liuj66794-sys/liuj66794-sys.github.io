押题卷三 Predicted Paper 3

全卷共 46 题：阅读理解 15 题 + 五选五 5 题 + 完形填空 15 题 + 语法填空 10 题 + 写作 1 篇 · 课件未注明考试时长，建议按正式考试节奏连续完成，总用时约 120 分钟。

使用说明本页不设自动倒计时，建议自行计时、按考试节奏连续完成；每个大题做完再展开答案核对；错题按解析里的考点回炉对应语法专题课。

第一部分 · 阅读理解 （第 1—15 题 · 建议 40 分钟）

共三篇文章。课件每题给出一处定位原文节选（原样照录，节选前后省略号为课件原样）；题目与选项按课件页序排列。

Passage A（第 1—5 题）

  1. 原文定位（首段）：

    …The oldest soap dates back to 3000 BCE, when people in Mesopotamia used it for cleaning the wool.

    What was the original purpose of soap in Mesopotamia?
    A. To wash faces.
    B. To wash dishes.
    C. To clean the wool.
    D. To clean the wood.

  2. 原文定位（第二段）：

    In the second century, a Greek doctor who was called Galen wrote about the practice of using soap to clean the body. However, bathing with soap was still not popular. It wasn't until the seventeenth century that soap was used for bathing in Europe, but only the rich people could afford it. In the nineteenth century, the soap became available to most people. As a result, cleanliness standards improved in many places.

    When did soap become available to most people?
    A. In the second century.
    B. In the third century.
    C. In the seventeenth century.
    D. In the nineteenth century.

  3. 原文定位（第三段）：

    …Because the soap still needs around two to three weeks to become hard enough before you can use it.

    How long does it need for the soap to become hard before you can use it?
    A. 1~2 days.
    B. 3~4 days.
    C. 1~2 weeks.
    D. 2~3 weeks.

  4. 原文定位（末段）：

    Being easy to make, soap has helped humans stay clean all the way to the modern age. You could even say it's one of the most important inventions in human history.

    Which of the following is NOT true according to the passage?
    A. It's very difficult to make soap.
    B. Soap is an important invention.
    C. Soap is extremely useful in life.
    D. Soap plays a crucial role in people's life.

  5. 原文定位（首段 / 第二段 / 第三段 / 末段）：

    Do you use soap every day? Do you know when people started using soap and how it is made? The word “soap” comes from the Celtic word “saipo”, which means “fat”, the material that soap is made from…

    In the second century, a Greek doctor who was called Galen wrote about the practice of using soap to clean the body…

    One reason that soap has been so popular throughout history is that it isn't hard to make…

    Being easy to make, soap has helped humans stay clean all the way to the modern age. You could even say it's one of the most important inventions in human history.

    Where is the passage most probably from?
    A. A recipe book.
    B. A health magazine.
    C. A science fiction.
    D. A political report.

Passage B（第 6—10 题）

  1. 原文定位（首段）：

    My mom's a big fan of posting pictures on her WeChat Moments: from the bright blue sky and lush green mountains to tranquil clear waters—these were what our family enjoyed when camping in a park during this summer holiday...

    According to the passage, what does the author's mother like?
    A. She likes singing and dancing very much.
    B. She likes traveling around the country very much.
    C. She likes posting pictures on her WeChat Moments.
    D. She likes making comments on her WeChat Moments.

  2. 原文定位（第三段）：

    With the mines closed, the environment has improved tremendously and the local people have found new ways to live. Many of our relatives there are now engaged in business, such as selling tea and running a hotel, and one third of the local population lives on tourism-related services. Their experiences truly prove that “green mountains are gold mountains”.

    Which of the following is NOT the present condition about the mother's hometown?
    A. The mines have been closed.
    B. Half of the local population lives on tourism-related services.
    C. The local people are trying their best to find new ways to live.
    D. Many of the author's relatives are now engaged in business.

  3. 原文定位（末段）：

    The past decade has witnessed a momentous shift in China's development strategies, and we've undertaken the path toward green development and a more beautiful China. For instance, eight years ago, Beijing won its bid to host the 2022 Winter Olympics and promised a greener Olympic Games. Last year, we enjoyed the most eco-friendly and sustainable Olympic Games in human history. These Chinese contributions to world environmental efforts represent China's wisdom and set an example for the globe.

    What does the word “momentous” (in Para. 4) mean in the passage?
    A. Necessary.
    B. Unimportant.
    C. Just so so.
    D. Very important.

  4. 原文定位（第二段）：

    My mom cherishes natural scenery and green environment because she comes from a small, poor village where people's life once depended on mining. When she was my age, the air there was so polluted that even the green bamboo leaves on the mountains were covered by black coal dust. In the past decade, however, things have changed.

    How does the mother feel about natural scenery and green environment?
    A. The mother loves natural scenery and green environment.
    B. The mother does not like natural scenery and green environment.
    C. The mother is indifferent to natural scenery and green environment.
    D. The mother thinks there is no difference between the past and the present.

  5. 原文定位（首段 / 第二段 / 末段）：

    …these were what our family enjoyed when camping in a park during this summer holiday...

    My mom cherishes natural scenery and green environment because she comes from a small, poor village where people's life once depended on mining. When she was my age, the air there was so polluted that even the green bamboo leaves on the mountains were covered by black coal dust. In the past decade, however, things have changed.

    The past decade has witnessed a momentous shift in China's development strategies，...

    Which of the following statements is TRUE according to the passage?
    A. The mother comes from a small city.
    B. China's development strategies have not changed greatly.
    C. The author's family camped in a park during this summer holiday.
    D. When the mother was young, the air in her hometown was not polluted.

Passage C（第 11—15 题）

  1. 原文定位（第二段）：

    The League for People with Disabilities was founded in June, 2016. The club opened exactly three years later and grew quickly, packing in many people from all over the state...

    When did Lucky Club open?
    A. In June, 2016.
    B. In July, 2016.
    C. In June, 2019.
    D. In July, 2019.

  2. 原文定位（首段）：

    …Hundreds of the disabled come to dance and make friends in Lucky Club. Some want to find love.Lucky Club attracts all of them because it has the sense of safety. It has the feeling that no one is judging them. They can be like anyone else out for the night.

    People with disabilities go to Lucky Club because _____.
    A. some relationships fail in the club
    B. they do not feel being judged there
    C. some medical problems can be solved there
    D. the attractive club event is held once a month

  3. 原文定位（第四段）：

    Sally Stern, 63, says, “Whatever you come looking for, you can find it here.” She uses a wheelchair after being paralyzed in 1994. She was hit by a car.“We see a lot of relationships blossom. Some fail. Love is always in the air here at Lucky Club. Everybody feels free here.”

    According to the passage, which is the most important reason why Sally Stern goes to Lucky Club?
    A. She likes strong club music.
    B. She wants to find love there.
    C. She feels love and freedom there.
    D. She wants to deal with mental disability.

  4. 原文定位（末段）：

    Organizers said most club-goers have intellectual or developmental disabilities. Roughly 1 in 6 is unable to speak. About 1 in 4 uses a wheelchair.

    What is the percentage of club-goers who use a wheelchair?
    A. 15%.
    B. 25%.
    C. 30%.
    D. 40%.

  5. 原文定位（首段 / 第三段 / 第四段）：

    …The League for People with Disabilities is transformed into a shining nightclub for disabled adults, Lucky Club...

    …Adults with disabilities, like Mike, yearn for more opportunities to socialize. Lucky Club stands out because it is held once a month and draws many people...

    …We see a lot of relationships blossom. Some fail...

    According to the passage, which of the following statements is NOT true?
    A. The disabled can go to the club every weekend.
    B. Some people do not successfully develop a relationship in the club.
    C. Adults with disabilities may get opportunities to socialize in the club.
    D. The nightclub is transformed from The League for People with Disabilities.

逐题解析

1. 答案 C　首段明确说美索不达米亚人最早用肥皂清洗羊毛。

2. 答案 D　第二段明确说到十九世纪肥皂才为大多数人所用。

3. 答案 D　第三段说肥皂还需约两至三周才能变硬并使用。

4. 答案 A　末段说肥皂易于制作，因此“很难制作”与原文不符。

5. 答案 B　文章介绍肥皂的起源、发展、制作和清洁作用，最可能来自健康杂志。

6. 答案 C　首段直接写母亲很喜欢在微信朋友圈发布照片。

7. 答案 B　原文说三分之一当地人口依靠旅游相关服务，并非一半。

8. 答案 D　momentous 表示“重大的、极其重要的”，与 very important 最接近。

9. 答案 A　cherishes 表明母亲珍爱自然景色和绿色环境。

10. 答案 C　首段明确提到作者一家这个暑假曾在公园露营。

11. 答案 C　该联盟成立于 2016 年 6 月，俱乐部恰好三年后开放，即 2019 年 6 月。

12. 答案 B　原文说俱乐部给人安全感，且没有人评判他们。

13. 答案 C　Sally 强调这里有爱且人人自由，因此最重要的原因是感受到爱与自由。

14. 答案 B　约四分之一的人使用轮椅，即 25%。

15. 答案 A　Lucky Club 每月举行一次，并非每个周末开放，故不正确的是 A。

第二部分 · 五选五 （第 16—20 题 · 建议 10 分钟）

Parents should help their children understand money. __16__ So you may start talking about money when your child shows an interest in buying things, candy or toys, for example.

1. The basic function of money

Being explaining the basic function of money by showing how people trade money for goods or services. It's important to show your child how money is traded for the thing he wants to have. If he wants to have a toy, give him the money and let him hand the money to the cashier. __17__ When your child grows a bit older and understands the basic function of money, you can start explaining more complex ways of using money.

2. Money lessons

Approach money lessons with openness and honesty. __18__ If you must say no to a child's request to spend money, explain, “You have enough toy trucks for now.” Or, if the request is for many different things, say, “You have to make a choice between this toy and that toy.”

3. __19__

Begin at the grocery store. Pick put similar brands of a product—a name brand butter and a generic（无商标产品）, for example. You can show your child how to make choices between different brands of a product so that you can save money. __20__ If he chooses the cheaper brand, allow him to make another purchase with the money saved. Later, you may explains how the more expensive choice leaves less money for other purchases.

A. Wise decision.
B. Tell the child why he can—or cannot—have certain things.
C. Permit the child to choose between them.
D. Talk about how the money bought the thing after you leave the toy store.
E. The best time to teach a child anything about money is when he shows an interest.

课件核对说明① 课件第 17 页将句中 talking 黄色高亮并用箭头重复标注（按笔记保留该标注）；② 原文存在 Being explaining、Pick put、may explains 等语言异常，题面忠实保留、未作改写，按通常语法可分别理解为 Begin explaining、Pick out、may explain；③ 课件第 17—18 页重复印有同一组选项 A—E，本页合并保留一次。

第四部分 · 语法填空 （第 36—45 题 · 建议 10 分钟）

Maxime and Lise are a young French couple. They are ready 36. ______（travel）across China from Beijing 37. ______ Yunnan Province on a tandem bicycle（双人自行车）. They have two main purposes and one of them is that they want to encourage green travel through it.

Maxime, 26, 38. ______（work）helping farmers raise their cows, while his wife Lise, 26, is a teacher. The two also like sharing their travel stories on Facebook.

“We choose a tandem bicycle because it makes it easier to communicate 39. ______ each other about interesting people and things we come across during the journey. Besides, bicycling is 40. ______（healthy）and it's a cheaper and more environment-friendly way of travelling than 41. ______（use）cars, motors and so on,” the couple said.

As for the other purpose of their journey, the two said they wanted to get people's good ideas on topics such as health, economy and politics. When they were asked why they 42. ______（choose）to visit China, they said it was because they often talked about its long history and 43. ______（beautiful）and how much of the country is competitive（有竞争力的）in the world.

“We know China is a country with a large population and will be the leader of the economy around the world in 44. ______ future,” they said.

The couple will be on 45. ______（they）journey to China soon. We hope they can make more people further know about China by sharing what they see and hear on the way. Meanwhile, we hope they enjoy their travel in China.

本节答案与解析（第 36—45 题）

答案速查（本节答案与解析为依据课件题面与语境核定的参考答案）：

36to travel37to
38works39with
40healthier41using
42chose43beauty
44the45their

逐题解析

36. 答案 to travel　be ready to do sth. 是固定结构。

37. 答案 to　from Beijing to Yunnan Province 构成 from ... to ...。

38. 答案 works　主语 Maxime 为第三人称单数，叙述一般事实用一般现在时。

39. 答案 with　communicate with each other 为固定搭配。

40. 答案 healthier　后有 than，healthy 应用比较级 healthier。

41. 答案 using　than 后与 travelling 的动作比较，用动名词 using。

42. 答案 chose　主句 were asked 和从句语境均为过去，填 chose。

43. 答案 beauty　与名词 history 并列，beautiful 应变为名词 beauty。

44. 答案 the　in the future 为固定搭配。

45. 答案 their　修饰名词 journey 应用形容词性物主代词 their。
