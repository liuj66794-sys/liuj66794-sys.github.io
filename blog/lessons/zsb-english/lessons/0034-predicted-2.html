全卷共 46 题：阅读理解 15 题 + 五选五 5 题 + 完形填空 15 题 + 语法填空 10 题 + 写作 1 篇 · 课件未注明考试时长，建议按正式考试节奏连续完成，总用时约 120 分钟。

使用说明本页不设自动倒计时，建议自行计时、按考试节奏连续完成；每个大题做完再展开答案核对；错题按解析里的考点回炉对应语法专题课。

第一部分 · 阅读理解 （第 1—15 题 · 建议 40 分钟）

共三篇文章。课件每题仅给出该题对应的原文段落，文章由各题所示段落按课件顺序拼合而成，括号内为课件所标段落位置（原样保留）；课件未展示的段落从略。

Passage A（第 1—5 题）

（首段）Since ancient times, frugality has been one of the traditional virtues among Chinese people. Following this traditional way of life, more young people in China are now looking to frugal, low-cost lifestyles in order to spend less and save more.

（第二段）They are beginning to realize and reject the pitfalls of consumerism, believing that simplicity can bring happiness and satisfaction.

（第三段）Economic and employment pressures, as well as changing attitudes toward material life, are also resulting in more young people turning their backs on extravagance and waste.

（第四段）Frugality is being practised in a number of ways. Some young people choose discount and low-priced goods, while others are replacing well-known brands with those that are cost-effective. Some are cautious when buying non-essential items, while others say they save all they can in order to make important purchases.

（课件未展示中间段落，从略。）

（末段）The student added, “As luxury food and brand-name clothing cannot increase my happiness, why not satisfy myself by spending less for the same amount of happiness?”

  1. According to the passage, what lifestyles are more young people expecting to live?
    A. Plain needy lifestyles.
    B. Rich high-cost lifestyles.
    C. Simple low-cost lifestyles.
    D. Extravagant pleasant lifestyles.
  2. What does the underlined word “They” (P2, L1) refer to?
    A. More young people in China.
    B. More young people in every country.
    C. More young people in any other countries.
    D. More young people in many other countries.
  3. Which is not the reason for more young people expecting a frugal life?
    A. Rich and wealthy life.
    B. Economic pressure.
    C. Employment pressures.
    D. Changing attitudes toward material life.
  4. How many ways of frugality are there being practised in the passage?
    A. 2.
    B. 3.
    C. 4.
    D. 5.
  5. What is the passage mainly about?
    A. More young people are in pursuit of happiness and satisfaction.
    B. More young people never buy luxury food and brand-name clothing.
    C. More young people have changed their attitudes toward consumerism.
    D. Frugal, low-cost lifestyles only attract a growing number of foreign followers.

Passage B（第 6—10 题）

（首段）His desire to put what he learned into practice increased after the university established a Luban Workshop in Ethiopia in 2021. The workshop was part of the professional training programs offered by China’s vocational education colleges with the aim of sharing expertise.

（第二段）To date, Chinese colleges have established 11 Luban Workshops in African countries, offering a wide range of professional training and help to the younger generation in these nations to build up their professional skills.

（课件未展示第三段，从略。）

（第四段）A total of 27 Luban Workshops have been established in 25 countries, most of them in the developing world. The programs provide training in vocational skills tailored to meet the demands of host countries.

（第五段）Most of the workshops are located at vocational colleges in host countries, and they are usually established through partnerships between such colleges in China and their local counterparts. The Chinese institutions share their equipment, teaching methods and materials, and provide training for teachers.

（第六段）Some African nations have already benefited from the vocational education cooperation programs. In Djibouti, the first 24 students trained by a Luban Workshop in fields such as railway operations and rail engineering technology have become interns for the Addis Ababa-Djibouti Railway.

（第七段）That workshop, the first of its kind launched by China in Africa, is expected to help train talent, which is in short supply, to ensure the maintenance and operations of the railway, a flagship project built by Chinese construction companies within the framework of the Belt and Road Initiative.

  1. Up to now, how many Luban Workshops have been set up in African countries?
    A. 11.
    B. 24.
    C. 25.
    D. 27.
  2. About Luban Workshops what is the purpose of the drive by China?
    A. To promote international cooperation on high education.
    B. To promote cross-institution cooperation on high education.
    C. To promote international cooperation on vocational education.
    D. To promote cross-institution cooperation on vocational education.
  3. According to the passage, which help is not offered by the Chinese colleges?
    A. Sharing equipment.
    B. Providing financial support.
    C. Providing training for teachers.
    D. Sharing teaching methods and materials.
  4. Why did China start the first Luban Workshop in Africa?
    A. To help maintain and operate the railway.
    B. To help train talent, which is in short supply.
    C. To help train students, which are in short supply.
    D. To help train senior engineers, which are in short supply.
  5. What is the passage trying to tell us?
    A. Luban Workshops are specially-made programs provided by China.
    B. Luban Workshops have offered degrees to students in Thailand.
    C. Luban Workshops have trained first 24 students in Djibouti.
    D. Luban Workshops have been beneficial to African vocational education.

Passage C（第 11—15 题）

（首段）Foreign and domestic businesses opened a total of 121 new flagship stores at major shopping centers in Shenzhen in the first half of the year as the city’s consumption made a strong recovery.

（第二段）Out of the 121 new stores, 40 are first in China and 34 in Shenzhen, signifying that the city has still retained its strong appeal to both foreign and domestic businesses, according to Winshang.

（课件未展示第三段，从略。）

（第四段）In the first half of the year, 38 internationally renowned brand flagship stores were launched, accounting for 31% of the new stores. This includes 31 international retail brands, three internationally renowned food and beverage brands and two hotel brands. These new stores have centered on catering, clothing, fashion and lifestyle sectors. Among them, fashion and lifestyle account for 24% of first stores, followed by clothing at 18% and leisure dining at 13%, Winshang data show.

（课件未展示第五段，从略。）

（第六段）Renowned global brands that open their flagship shops in Shenzhen will receive a reward of 1 million yuan (US $138,100),…

（第七段）…Among the measures, property owners that introduced flagship shops of renowned domestic and global brands will receive rewards of 500,000 yuan for retail outlets, or 200,000 yuan for catering outlets.

  1. What makes it possible for the 121 new flagship stores to open in Shenzhen?
    A. The city can provide data for these foreign and domestic businesses.
    B. The city’s consumption has made a steady and strong increase.
    C. The city’s consumption mainly depends on foreign and domestic businesses.
    D. The city is famous as an international consumption center in South China.
  2. 34 stores open in Shenzhen for the first time, which shows ______.
    A. its strong appeal
    B. its leading innovation
    C. its proper policy
    D. its driving plan
  3. What is the meaning of the word “catering” (in Para.4)?
    A. The work of providing make-up.
    B. The work of providing costume.
    C. The work of providing entertainment.
    D. The work of providing food and drinks.
  4. Among these new stores, which sector accounts for the highest percentage of first stores?
    A. Catering.
    B. Clothing.
    C. Leisure activity.
    D. Lifestyle.
  5. Which of the following is NOT related to “first store economy” measures?
    A. To reward famous global brands for opening their flagship shops in Shenzhen.
    B. To reward Shenzhen’s commerce bureau for its contribution.
    C. To reward property owners for introducing flagship shops of famous domestic brands.
    D. To reward property owners for introducing flagship shops of famous global brands.

7. 答案 C　鲁班工坊旨在推动职业教育国际合作。

8. 答案 B　设备、教学方法材料及师资培训均有，未提财政支持。

9. 答案 B　设立工坊的直接目的为培养短缺人才，保障铁路维护运营。

10. 答案 D　全文说明鲁班工坊为非洲职业教育带来实际益处。

15. 答案 B　措施奖励品牌和引进品牌的物业方，未奖励商务局本身。

第二部分 · 五选五 （第 16—20 题 · 建议 10 分钟）

Kids’ health: Four steps for fighting stress

Everybody gets stressed time to time. __16__ Some ways of dealing with stress—like screaming or hitting someone—don’t solve much. But other ways, like talking to someone you trust, can lead you to solving your problem or at least feeling better.

Try taking these four steps the next time you are stressed:

Get support. When you need help, reach out to the people who care about you. Talk to a trusted adult, such as a parent or other relatives. __17__ They might have had similar problems, such as dealing with a test, or the death of a beloved pet.

Don’t take it out on yourself. Sometimes when kids are stressed and upset they take it out on themselves. Oh, dear, that’s no good idea. Remember that there are always people to help you. Don’t take it out on yourself. __18__

Try to solve the problem. After you’re calm and you have support from adults and friends, it’s time to get down to business. __19__ Even if you can’t solve it all, you can solve a piece of it.

Be positive. Most stress is temporary. Remember stress does go away, especially when you figure out the problem and start working on solving it. These steps aren’t magic, but they do work. And if you can stay positive as you make your way through a tough time, you’ll help yourself feel better even faster. __20__

A. Ask for a helping hand to get you through the tough situation.
B. Different people feel stress in different ways.
C. Ah, it feels so good when the stress is gone.
D. You need to figure out what the problem is.
E. And don’t forget about your friends.

逐题解析

36. 答案 tenth　复合形容词所需序数词为 tenth。考点：序数词构成复合形容词（回炉 0005 课 · 数词和连词）

37. 答案 hoping　主句动作伴随希望看见星星，用现在分词 hoping。考点：现在分词作状语（回炉 0011 课 · 非谓语）

38. 答案 of　be afraid of 为固定搭配。

39. 答案 softly　修饰动词 sings 应用副词 softly；图片题面明确给出 (soft)。考点：副词修饰动词（回炉 0006 课 · 形容词和副词）

40. 答案 warmth　形容词性物主代词 her 后接名词 warmth。

41. 答案 kisses　一般现在时主语 She，谓语用 kisses。考点：一般现在时第三人称单数（回炉 0009 课 · 时态语态 / 0010 课 · 主谓一致）

42. 答案 as　as usual 为固定短语。

43. 答案 told　is told 构成一般现在时被动语态。考点：一般现在时被动语态（回炉 0009 课 · 时态语态）

44. 答案 for　be late for school 为固定搭配。

45. 答案 Thousands　thousands of 表示“成千上万”，句首首字母大写。考点：thousands of 不确切数量（回炉 0005 课 · 数词和连词）
