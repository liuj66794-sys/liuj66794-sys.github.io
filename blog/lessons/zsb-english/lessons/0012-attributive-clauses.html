语法专题 · 第十二课

定语从句 Attributive Clauses

对应教材上册 P104–P114 · 考关系词选择（先行词 + 从句缺不缺成分）、只能用 that/which 的情况、限制性 vs 非限制性、as 引导与主谓一致。

考点地图 · 点击跳转
  1. 什么是定语与定语从句：先行词 + 引导词 + 从句；关系词三大作用
  2. 关系代词 6 个：that / which / who / whom / whose / as（+ 省略规则）
  3. 介词 + which/whom：介词提前、选介词、两种常见形式
  4. 只能用 that / 只能用 which / 常用 who 的情况
  5. 关系副词 3 个：when / where / why；缺不缺成分定关系词；抽象地点与 way
  6. 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句 + as 引导的定语从句
  7. 定语从句的主谓一致：one of vs the only one of

从句三大类：名词性从句（主语/表语/宾语/同位语）、定语从句（形容词性从句，本课）、状语从句（副词性从句：时间/地点/原因/目的/方式/条件/比较/让步/结果）。

第一部分 · 什么是定语与定语从句

1. 什么是定语

  • 含义：译为"……的"。
  • 作用：修饰限定名词或代词。
  • 位置：形容词作定语多置于名词之前；短语/句子作定语置于名词之后。

a beautiful pen
the boy in the classroom（教室里的男孩）
apples on the tree（树上的苹果）
That boy who has long bangs is Tom.（那个留着长刘海的男生是 Tom）

2. 什么是定语从句

从句就是带引导词的一句话。定语从句 = 放在名词之后、翻译成"……的"的、带引导词的一句话。

口诀定语从句 = 放在名词后、译"……的"、带引导词的一句话；结构：n.（先行词）+ 引导词 + 从句
划分从句的方法 从引导词开始划，划到引导词后的第二个谓语动词之前（若引导词后只有一个谓语动词，则划到句子最后）：

What we need is patience.
The fact is that he is singing now.
Whether the project will succeed depends on teamwork.
She told me that she would call later.
The news that the team won the championship spread quickly.

3. 基本概念与关系词的作用

先行词：被定语从句所修饰的名词、代词；关系词：引导定语从句的词，包括关系代词和关系副词。

关系词的三大作用：连接 + 指代先行词 + 在定语从句中担当一个成分：

This is the book that I bought yesterday.（先行词 the book / 关系词 that / 定语从句 that I bought yesterday）

第二部分 · 关系代词（教材 P105–110）

关系代词先行词从句中所作成分
that人、物主语、宾语、表语
which物、整句话主语、宾语、表语
who人（包括人名）主语、宾语、表语
whom人（包括人名）宾语
whose人、物定语
as人、物、整句话主语、宾语、表语
关系词成员
关系代词that、which、who、whom、whose、as
关系副词when、where、why
省略规则关系代词在从句中作宾语时可省略；作主语时不可省略。
关系代词用法要点例句
that人、物皆可She is the girl that lives next door.
He is the man (that) I want to see.
The building that stands near the river is our school.
The letter (that) I received yesterday was from him.
which物The building which/that stands near the river is our school.
指代整句话The sun heats the earth, which is very important to us. 太阳加热地球，这对我们很重要。
who人（包括人名）She is the girl who/that lives next door.
He is the man (who/that) I want to see.
whom人，宾格，只作宾语She's the girl (whom/who/that) I told you about.
He is the man (whom/who/that) I want to see.
whose人、物，作定语（本质是先行词的所有格）The building whose windows face south was built last year.
= The building the windows of which face south was built last year.
as人、物、整句话As is known to the world, Mark Twain is a great American writer. 众所周知，马克·吐温是伟大的美国作家。
This is such a difficult problem as no one can solve. 这是一道难到没人能解决的题。
whose 替换公式whose + n. = the + n. + of which（指物）= the + n. + of whom（指人）
the same...as vs the same...that the same...as：与……相似：I have bought the same watch as you have.（很像，但不是同一块）
the same...that：与……相同：This is the same watch that I lost.（同一块）

第三部分 · 介词 + which/whom 引导的定语从句（教材 P107）

1. 介词提前

当 which 或 whom 充当定语从句中介词的宾语时，介词可以提前到 which/whom 之前：

I still remember the day on which we first met. 我仍然记得我们第一次见面的那一天。（从句原本 which we first met on，介词 on 提前）
The scientist from whom we learned a lot passed away last month. 我们从他那里学到很多东西的那位科学家上个月去世了。（从句原本 whom we learned a lot from）

2. 介词后用 which 还是 whom

看先行词——人用 whom，物用 which：

The girl with whom I went to the party is my cousin. 和我一起去派对的那个女孩是我的表妹。
This is the book about which we talked yesterday. 这就是我们昨天谈论的那本书。

3. 介词的选择

  • 根据介词与先行词的固定搭配：I will never forget the day on which Tom came to this company.（on the day 在那一天）
  • 根据从句谓语动词的固定搭配：This is the smartphone on which he spent 5000 yuan.（spend + 金钱 + on sth.）

4. 两种常见形式

  • whose + n. = the + n. + of which/whom：This is the book the cover of which is designed by Mr. Jones. = This is the book whose cover is designed by Mr. Jones.
  • all/some/both/most of which(whom)：He attended the meeting with many other experts, most of whom came from the US. 他和许多其他专家一起参加了会议，他们大多数来自美国。

第四部分 · 只能用 that / 只能用 which / 常用 who（教材 P108–110）

情况例句
只能用 that（五种）
1. 先行词由形容词最高级或序数词修饰，或先行词本身就是序数词、最高级This is the best film that I have seen.
This is the first film that I have seen since I came here.
2. 先行词被 the very（正是）/ the only / the same / the last 修饰This is the very book that I am looking for.
3. 先行词是 all, much, anything, something, nothing, everything, little, none 等不定代词，或被 all, every, no, some, any, little, much 修饰All that can be done has been done. 所能做的一切都已经做了。
4. 先行词前面有 who, which 等疑问代词Who is the man that is standing there?
5. 先行词既有人也有物The writer and his work that you told me are really famous.
只能用 which（两种）
1. 介词后面用 which 不用 that（除了固定搭配 in that "因为"）The building in which Han Mei studied was very old.
2. 非限制性定语从句中用 which 不用 thatCrusoe's dog, which was very old now, became ill and died.
常用 who 不用 that（两种）
1. 先行词是指人的不定代词（anybody, those 等）Those who have not got your textbooks please raise your hands.
2. 非限制性定语从句中先行词指人，只能用 whoMy father, who is a doctor, works very hard.
口诀只能 that：最序、唯同最、不定代、疑问前、人物齐

第五部分 · 关系副词（教材 P111–112）

关系副词先行词在从句中所作成分
when时间时间状语，相当于介词 + which
where地点地点状语，相当于介词 + which
why原因（先行词一般为 the reason(s)）原因状语，相当于 for which

等价替换：where = in which / on which / at which / to which...；when = on which / in which / at which / during which...；why = for which。

拓展 1 · 时间/地点/原因名词后不一定用关系副词

关键看从句缺不缺主要成分：缺主要成分 → 用关系代词；不缺主要成分 → 用关系副词。

例句判断
I'll never forget the days when I worked together with you.不缺成分 → when
I'll never forget the days that/which I spent together with you.spent 缺宾语 → 关系代词
This is the house where we lived fifteen years ago.不缺成分 → where
This is the house that/which we sold fifteen years ago.sold 缺宾语 → 关系代词
The reason why he is late is known to all.不缺成分 → why
The reason that/which he gave me is not convincing.gave 缺宾语 → 关系代词

拓展 2 · where 的先行词包括抽象地点

常见抽象地点名词：situation（情况）、point（情况，地步）、case（情况）、stage（阶段）、position（位置）、atmosphere（气氛）、platform（平台）、occasion（场合）等。

He got himself into a dangerous situation where he is likely to lose control over the plane. 他使自己陷入一种危险的境地，在这种情况下他很可能失去对飞机的控制。

拓展 3 · 先行词是 way 的时候

  • 从句缺主要成分时，用 that/which，从句缺宾语时可省略引导词：We first broke the law in a way which/that was peaceful. 我们第一次以一种和平的方式违法了。The way (that/which) he told us is useful. 他告诉我们的方法是有用的。
  • 从句不缺主要成分时，用 that / in which / 省略：I don't like the way (that/in which) you talked to your mum. 我不喜欢你和你妈妈说话的方式。

第六部分 · 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句（教材 P113–114）

区别限制性定语从句非限制性定语从句
形式与主句之间不用逗号隔开与主句之间一般用逗号隔开
功能对先行词进行限定、修饰，去掉后剩余部分意义不完整、不明确对先行词作附加说明，去掉后句子剩余部分意思仍完整、明确
先行词名词或名词性词组名词、名词性词组或整个主句
引导词所有的关系代词/副词除 that 和 why 之外的关系代词/副词

I have two sons who work at school. 我有两个在学校工作的儿子。（可能不止两个儿子——限定）
I have two sons, who work at school. 我有两个儿子，他们在学校工作。（就两个——附加说明）

which 在非限中先行词可以是指物的词，也可以是一句话：

These apple trees, which I planted three years ago, have not borne any fruit. 这些苹果树是我三年前种的，它们还没有结出任何果实。
He said that he had never seen her before, which was not true. 他说他以前从未见过她，这不是真的。

第七部分 · as 引导的定语从句（教材 P114）

1. as 和 which 引导非限制性定语从句的区别

  • as 可以引导非限制性定语从句放在句首、句中，表示"正如……"
  • which 引导的非限制性定语从句一般放于主句后或句中，没有"正如"的意思，一般翻译为"这"

As was expected, she has married again. 正如所料，她又结婚了。（She has married again, as was expected.）
The sun heats the earth, which is very important to us. 太阳加热地球，这对我们很重要。

口诀非限不用 that/why；as 放句首"正如"，which 放句尾译"这"

2. as 引导的非限制性定语从句常见固定搭配

as is often the case（情况常常如此）、as we all know（众所周知）、as is mentioned above（正如上面所提到的那样）、as has been said before（如前所述）、as is reported（据报道）

第八部分 · 定语从句的主谓一致

定语从句中，从句动词的单复数取决于前面的名词。公式：n. + 引导词 + ________（填动词）：He is the student that does well in study.

  • one of + 复数名词 + 定从：从句谓语用复数：He is one of the students that pass the exam.
  • one of 前有 the only 修饰：从句谓语用单数：He is the only one of the students that passes the exam.
口诀选关系词灵魂三问：先行词是人/物/时间/地点/原因？从句缺什么成分？限不限非限？

易错清单

高频易错 1. 看从句缺不缺成分选关系词：缺主/宾 → 关系代词；不缺 → 关系副词（时间 when / 地点 where / 原因 why）
2. 只能用 that 五情况：最高级/序数词、the very/only/same/last、不定代词、疑问词开头、人+物
3. 介词后只用 which/whom，不用 that（除固定搭配 in that 因为）
4. 非限不能用 that 和 why；指物用 which，指人用 who
5. the same...as 相似 / the same...that 同一物
6. as 可放句首表"正如"（As is known to all），which 不行；which 可指代整句话翻译"这"
7. 抽象地点（situation/case/point/stage/position/atmosphere/platform/occasion）后用 where
8. 先行词是 way：不缺成分 → that/in which/省略；缺 → that/which
9. 主谓一致：one of + 复数 + 定从 → 复数；the only one of → 单数
