整卷五大部分：阅读理解 15 题＋五选五 5 题＋完形填空 15 题＋语法填空 10 题＋写作 1 题，共 46 题；课件未注明各部分分值与用时，建议总用时 120 分钟。

使用说明本页不设自动倒计时，建议自行计时、按考试节奏连续完成；每个大题做完再展开答案核对；错题建议回炉延伸阅读笔记中的对应考点与例题。

第一部分 · 阅读理解（第 1—15 题）（建议 40 分钟）

从每题所给的四个选项中选出最佳选项。

  1. According to the passage, which of the following is NOT proved in the studies of learning chess?

    ② You could get better scores in math by learning chess. I found many pages on the Internet telling how this game is so much more than just a way to pass the time. Chess requires problem solving. Educators and researchers have done studies with students just like you and me. These studies prove that chess teaches how to think ahead, how to plan, and how to be systematic in an approach to problem solving. If we know better how to use these skills, it figures we can use these same techniques to solve math problems. I read that one professor said that he saw improvement in his math students’ scores after they had been playing chess for only three weeks. Is there any one of us who couldn’t improve his or her math skills?

    A. How to think ahead.
    B. How to plan.
    C. How to be systematic in an approach to problem solving.
    D. How to use math techniques.
  2. What is the meaning of “mental workout”?

    ③ Playing chess is a mental workout. It is thinking and analyzing. When we read, we think about and analyze the material and hope that we comprehend (理解) it. Playing chess also will help us learn to concentrate something we must do when we read.

    A. Thinking and playing.
    B. Thinking and analyzing.
    C. Playing and analyzing.
    D. Reading and analyzing.
  3. How much would you pay for learning chess?

    ④ There is no cost for chess lessons. There is no special equipment to buy or uniform required. The only thing you have to bring is a determination to learn how to play.

    A. No money
    B. Some money
    C. Cost for a special equipment.
    D. Cost for a uniform.
  4. For what purpose might the author have written the passage?

    ① I am happy to have the chance to talk to you during our class meeting today. If I told you that playing a particular game would give a boost to your reading skills, would you want to learn the game? Sure you would. The game I am talking about is chess.

    A. As a report for a newspaper.
    B. As an essay for English class.
    C. As a speech to classmates.
    D. As a letter to a professor.
  5. What is the main idea of the passage?

    ⑤ Strategy and reasoning are tools we can use for a lifetime. Chess will help us develop these skills! Welcome to our chess lesson!

    A. Chess is an important game in the world.
    B. Chess raises math scores because it helps to teach problem solving
    C. It is not difficult to learn chess, and it can be played online.
    D. Because chess teaches various skills, it will be beneficial to have chess lessons.
  6. What happened while Horner was driving one day?

    ① One day, Justin Horner was driving down a busy freeway in Portland, Oregon, when his car broke down. He pulled over to the side of the road and made a sign that said he needed help. Three hours later, a van finally pulled up. Out came a family of three. Then the father and Horner worked together to fix the car.

    A. His car stopped working.
    B. A family asked him for help.
    C. A van ran into his car
    D. He hit a road sign.
  7. What do we know about the mother from Paragraph 2?

    ② When they finished, the mom pulled out a big jug of water, and they drank and washed their hands. Horner recalled, “I tried to give her money. I only had a $20 bill, and I just thought, you know, it’s the least I could do. And she just wouldn’t take it.” But Horner insisted, and eventually he put the money in her hand, and walked away.

    A. She walked away.
    B. She had little money.
    C. She tried to refuse the bill
    D. She drank a big jug of water.
  8. Why did the daughter give Horner a tamale?

    ③ Then Horner heard the daughter call out, to ask him if he was hungry. Indeed he was, and she came over with a tamale from their cooler. They exchanged thank-yous, she got into the van, and the family started to drive away. “As they’re trying to get into traffic, I unwrapped the tamale and my money is in it,” Horner said.

    A. To cool him down.
    B. To make him happy.
    C. To exchange thanks.
    D. To return his money.
  9. What did the father mean when he said “Today you, tomorrow me”?

    ④ Holding the bill out, Horner immediately ran over to the van to get the father’s attention. “He rolls down his window. He sees me coming and he is just shaking his head,” Horner remembered. “And he just kind of puts his hand up and he just, you know, with this big smile on his face, he just says, ‘Today you, tomorrow me.’” The man then gave Horner a wave, rolled up the window, and drove off. The last thing Horner saw was the young girl waving goodbye through the window. He never saw them again.

    A. We are friends.
    B. Time is money.
    C. Everyone needs help.
    D. Accidents occur daily.
  10. How long did Justin Horner wait for help before the family in the van stopped?

    ① One day, Justin Horner was driving down a busy freeway in Portland, Oregon, when his car broke down. He pulled over to the side of the road and made a sign that said he needed help. Three hours later, a van finally pulled up. Out came a family of three. Then the father and Horner worked together to fix the car.

    A. One hour
    B. Two hours
    C. Three hours
    D. Four hours
  11. The man once thought advertising was ______.

    ① A man once said how useless it was to put advertisements in the newspapers. “Last week,” he said, “my umbrella was stolen from a London church. As it was a present, I spent twice its worth in advertising, but didn’t get it back.”

    A. of little use
    B. of some use
    C. practical
    D. valuable
  12. The result of the first advertisement was that ______.

    ① A man once said how useless it was to put advertisements in the newspapers. “Last week,” he said, “my umbrella was stolen from a London church. As it was a present, I spent twice its worth in advertising, but didn’t get it back.”

    A. the man got his umbrella back
    B. the man wasted some money advertising
    C. nobody found the missing umbrella
    D. the umbrella was found somewhere near the church
  13. The merchant suggested that the man should ______.

    ④ “Now,” said the merchant, “I often advertise, and find that it pays me well. But the way in which an advertisement is expressed is of extreme importance. Let us try for your umbrella again, and if it fails, I’ll buy you a new one.”

    A. buy a new umbrella
    B. go on looking for his umbrella
    C. write another better advertisement
    D. report to the police
  14. Why did the merchant say “I often advertise and find that it pays me well”?

    ④ “Now,” said the merchant, “I often advertise, and find that it pays me well. But the way in which an advertisement is expressed is of extreme importance. Let us try for your umbrella again, and if it fails, I’ll buy you a new one.”

    A. He knew how to advertise.
    B. He had more money for advertising.
    C. He found it easy to advertise.
    D. He had a friend in the newspaper where he advertised.
  15. This is a story about _____.

    ⑤ The merchant then took a slip of paper out of his pocket and wrote: “If the man who was seen to take an umbrella from the City Church last Sunday evening doesn’t wish to get into trouble, he will return the umbrella to No.10 Broad Street. He is well known.”

    ⑥ This appeared in the paper, and on the following morning, the man was astonished when he opened the front door. In the doorway lay at least twelve umbrellas of all sizes and colors that had been thrown in, and his own was among the number. Many of them had notes fastened to them saying that they had been taken by mistake, and begging the loser not to say anything about the matter.

    A. a useless advertisement
    B. how to make an effective advertisement
    C. how the man lost and found his umbrella
    D. what the merchant did for the umbrella owner

第二部分 · 五选五（第 16—20 题）（建议 10 分钟）

从下列备选句子中为文章各空格选出最佳选项（共 5 个空格、5 个备选句子）。

Sleep is something we all do. But some people need to sleep more than others. Babies sleep most of the time. Children in school sleep about ten to twelve hours a night. Most grown-ups sleep only seven or eight hours. __16__

All parts of our bodies have to rest after they work. Our arms need a rest after we lift heavy things. __17__ They get tired. We have to rest them. Our brains work hard, too. When we are awake, they help us pay attention to the world around us. We can sit quite still and rest our arms and legs. But our brains aren’t resting. They go right on thinking as long as we are awake. Our brains slow down a bit when we sleep and dream. But even as we sleep, our brains are doing some work. Instead of thinking wide-awake thoughts, our brains make up dreams. __18__ Some are not. Most of the time we forget them when we wake up.

Scientists have tried to find out what would happen if people were not allowed to sleep. They asked some people not to go to bed. The people stayed up all night and all the next day. They stayed up the next night, too, and the day after. They became very sleepy. They tried to read, but they could not follow the words. They played games, but they made mistakes. They forgot things. __19__ These people grew rude and mean. They became angry with their friends. Finally they were too tired to stand up. The moment they sat down, they fell asleep.

__20__ But we do know that we need it to stay well. So tonight have a good sleep. Lie down under the covers. Shut your eyes. Let your thoughts travel about. Soon you’ll stop thinking. You’ll be asleep.

第 36 题 · 答案：difference（参考答案，整理时补充）　considerable 修饰名词；谓语 is 要求单数 difference。

第 37 题 · 答案：comes（参考答案，整理时补充）　when it comes to 是“谈到……”的固定表达。

第 38 题 · 答案：to open（参考答案，整理时补充）　it is not polite to do sth. 中不定式是真正主语。

第 39 题 · 答案：left（参考答案，整理时补充）　after 引导的从句描述离开已完成，用现在完成时 has left。

第 40 题 · 答案：carefully（参考答案，整理时补充）　修饰动词 decorate 应用副词 carefully。

第 41 题 · 答案：with（参考答案，整理时补充）　decorate sth. with sth. 表示“用……装饰……”。

第 42 题 · 答案：expected（参考答案，整理时补充）　receiver 与 expect 为被动关系，构成 is expected to do。

第 43 题 · 答案：curiosity（参考答案，整理时补充）　and 连接 eagerness 与另一个名词，故填 curiosity。

第 44 题 · 答案：what（参考答案，整理时补充）　what 引导表语从句并在从句中作 wanted 的宾语。

Dear Amber,

I’m glad to know that you are interested in the position of foreign volunteer in the city museum. I surely approve of this idea because this job not only is beneficial to your Chinese study but also enables you to learn more about the history and culture of the city.

Before the selection, you’d better prepare a brief self-introduction, where you can show your love and understanding of Chinese culture. It’s also helpful to learn something about the museum in advance, such as its exhibitions and activities. You can visit its website or go to the museum in person.

Feel free to turn to me if there are any further questions. I hope you can pass the selection successfully and have a good experience there.

Yours,
Li Hua
