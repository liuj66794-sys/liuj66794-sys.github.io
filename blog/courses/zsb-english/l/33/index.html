Passage A（第 1—5 题）

（首段）With China’s rapid development in artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, more companies have noted the huge business potential in AI companionship sectors, as simulated AI pets with adorable appearances are gaining increasing popularity among Chinese consumers.

（第二段）Zhang Yi, CEO of the iiMedia Research Institute, said that consumers’ demand for emotional support and the capability of current AI technologies offer this type of product greater business potential.

（第三段）A 20-year-old student named Wang Li, who is also an AI technology fan, said that she bought “Boo Boo”, a simulated AI robotic pet developed by Hangzhou-based Genmoor Technology.

（第四段）“I first learned about the product during a major assignment investigation and remembered it,” said Wang Li.

（第五段）“I decided to buy it out of curiosity, but especially because of its adorable fluffy appearance and the color of its fur, which is just like my dog’s,” Wang Li added.

（课件未展示第六段，从略。）

（第七段）Wang Li stated that the advantage of having an AI pet is that it doesn’t require extra spending on food or time for walks every day, while noting that it still lacks interaction flexibility and real companionship when compared with real dogs or cats.

（末段）Zhang Yi predicted more possibilities in applying AI technologies in emotional support and companionship robots, with target users covering all age groups, and whose functions include the ability to detect users’ potential physical and mental health issues.

  1. The huge business potential has been noticed by more companies in _____. A. positive feedback B. real pets like dogs or cats C. AI companionship sectors D. popularity among consumers
  2. When did Wang Li learn about the simulated AI pets? A. During her survey related to her major. B. During her visit to Genmoor Technology. C. During her work in iiMedia Research Institute. D. During her surfing e-commerce platform Taobao.
  3. Wang Li decided to buy Boo Boo for the following reasons EXCEPT _____. A. her curiosity B. the color of its fur C. its lovely appearance D. its emotional support
  4. The disadvantage of having an AI pet is that _____. A. it needs careful maintenance and power charge B. it lacks interaction flexibility and real companionship C. it can detect users’ potential physical and mental health issues D. it requires no extra spending on food or time for walks every day
  5. What’s the passage mainly about? A. Simulated AI pets have huge business potential. B. AI pets are applied in every aspect of life. C. Simulated pets will sell well and be restocked frequently. D. Boo Boo, a simulated AI robotic pet, is perfect without any disadvantages.

Passage B（第 6—10 题）

（首段）Guochao, literally meaning “national tide”, refers to modern products packed with traditional Chinese cultural elements. In recent years, it has experienced a rise because of a growing interest among younger generations in their cultural heritage and a desire to integrate it into their daily lives. Many platforms now show traditional crafts, fashion and art, making them more accessible and appealing.

（第二段）For example, with the arrival of the year of the snake, the image of the snake is integrated into various products such as hongbao, the red envelope that older generations fill with money and give to young people as a symbol of good fortune…Guochao products combine traditional cultural elements with modern design.

（第三段）Shanxi’s cultural tourism has rapidly gained popularity because of the global hit online game Black Myth: Wukong. As many scenes in the game are created based on cultural and historical sites in Shanxi, the province is witnessing a remarkable surge in tourists, who are either the fans of the game or fans interested in the ancient architecture and sculptures and the stories behind them. The Guochao craze provides Shanxi with vast opportunities for innovation. The local government integrates Guochao elements into scenic area development and tourism souvenir creation. A line of refrigerator magnets shaped like the architecture in the game have been developed to increase the cultural value of tourism.

  1. Guochao has experienced a rise because _____. A. younger generations are fond of buying tourism souvenirs B. younger generations are crazy about hongbao during the Spring Festival C. younger generations are addicted to the online game Black Myth: Wukong D. younger generations are interested in cultural heritage and eager to integrate it into their daily lives
  2. What does the underlined word “appealing” mean in paragraph 1? A. Supportive. B. Protective. C. Attractive. D. Detective.
  3. According to the passage, which is a proper description of hongbao in China? A. It is a red envelope filled with notes and given to the young as a symbol of good wish. B. It is a red envelope filled with gifts and given to the young as a symbol of good luck. C. It is a red envelope filled with money and given to the young as a symbol of good fortune. D. It is a red envelope filled with objects and given to the young as a symbol of good souvenirs.
  4. Which is not TRUE about “Guochao” products? A. They all have the theme of the snake. B. They have modern design concepts. C. They involve traditional cultural elements. D. They can satisfy consumers’ pursuit of fashion.
  5. It can be inferred from the last paragraph that _____. A. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism lies in its commercials on media B. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism is due to the online game Black Myth: Wukong C. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism mainly owes to the hospitality of the local people D. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism relies on its ancient architecture and sculptures

Passage C（第 11—15 题）

（首段）As China’s visa-free travel policy continues to expand and facilitate easier exchanges between people, the attraction of “China Travel” becomes stronger.

（第二段）Francisco Lopez, a tourist from Spain, shared his experiences with the Global Times on Sunday, highlighting the ease of his trip to China made possible by the country’s visa-free policy and other supporting measures. During this trip, he visited Beijing, Xi’an, Guilin, and Shanghai… “We didn’t have cash and didn’t exchange any money. We used Alipay everywhere, and it worked perfectly,” he added.

（第三段）To meet the needs of foreign tourists, various regions and tourist destinations in China are enhancing the tourism experience. A worker from the Wulingyuan scenic area in Zhangjiajie of central China’s Hunan Province told the media that the visa-free policy has significantly boosted the tourism market, with a notable increase in the number of foreign visitors. “There is a shift in tourists, with more coming from southeast Asia and Europe thanks to the implementation of visa-free policies,” another employee said.

（第四段）In the first three quarters of this year, Zhangjiajie City received over 1.29 million inbound tourists, a year-on-year increase of 255.41 percent. The city achieved inbound tourism revenue of $358.54 million, marking a year-on-year growth of 307.61 percent, according to official data.

  1. Francisco Lopez visited the following cities EXCEPT _____. A. Beijing B. Xi’an C. Zhangjiajie D. Guili
  2. Why didn’t Francisco Lopez exchange any money during his trip to China? A. Because he did not have cash. B. Because he got everything for free. C. Because China’s e-payment system is available everywhere. D. Because China’s visa-free policy facilitates easier exchanges between people.
  3. What does the underlined word “notable” mean in paragraph 3? A. Reliable. B. Remarkable. C. Variable. D. Terrible.
  4. What can be inferred from this passage? A. The visa-free policies will cause traffic jams in big cities. B. The visa-free policies influenced Chinese people’s life. C. The visa-free policies made the domestic tourists happy. D. The visa-free policies brought about the growth of inbound tourists.
  5. What is the main idea of this passage? A. Tourist destinations in China enhance tourism experience. B. More inbound tourists come from southeast Asia and Europe. C. A Spanish tourist shared his experience of China’s e-payment system. D. The visa-free policy has significantly boosted China’s tourism market.
